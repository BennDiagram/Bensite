I'm the guitarist and manager of a funk band called Funky Lamp. I also work with a local organization called Post Genre where I am responsible for our visual branding and web design/web development. We are currently preparing to open Oakland's first independent music venue in January 2025, and the pre-launch website can be found here! I've been a visual artist all my life, and have had the opportunity to apply this intuition in many of my projects.

In my free time, you'll find me cooking, reading, or endlessly scrolling Facebook Marketplace. At one time, I was also a 3D Prop & Environment artist. Lastly, as you can probably see, I mess around with web development.

FUTURE PLANS

The way things are going, my future plan is just a collection of Plan B's. My "main" plan B is to stay in Pittsburgh. I have an EXTREMELY frugal lifestyle, and would be able to support myself working 20 hours/week. That's, like, 2 double-shifts a week. 20 hours a week is the minimum amount I would need to receive food stamps, which would cushion my earnings and give me the flexibility to still pout away around $300 a month. This would buy me a massive amount of time and energy to pursue certifications in business analytics, product development, cybersecurity, GIS, or whatever else I decide to pursue. Ideally, after 6 months of dedication to certifications, I would build enough of a skillset to make myself employable in an industry related to my degrees and, hopefully, secure white-collar employment.

Obviously, this is just one possible outcome. I could, in the next year, find a suitcase full of cocaine/get an internship/etc. that would put me on a better trajectory. But for now, I'm finding some comfort in preparing for the worst. Sorry for the pessimism. Thanks for listening.
